Two layers: what the buyer sees at checkout and in the ticket email (§1), and the internal rules the door and the inbox follow (§2).

1. Buyer-facing terms (checkout page + ticket email + fine print on the event page)

Tickets

  • Testing Pass, Saturday Oct 10, $200. 100 available.
  • Testing Pass, Sunday Oct 11, $200. 100 available.
  • Preparty VIP, Friday Oct 9, $200. Includes a QCB flat top banger, a Hitman bag and more. 20 available.
  • Preparty GA, Friday Oct 9, $20.
  • Saturday and Sunday are separate passes. $200 buys one day. Want both days, buy both passes. The preparty is its own ticket and is not included in any pass.
  • Saturday After Party, Oct 10, 8:30 PM to late. Its own ticket, not included in the testing pass. Announced as a second drop after the initial passes are on sale. Price TBD.
  • Max 10 passes per day per order. Presale only. Nothing is sold at the door by anyone.

Who can buy and who gets in

  • 21+. Government ID at the door, and the name on the ID must match the name on the ticket.
  • Private event. You are buying access to a private, invitation-only gathering. Your ticket is your invitation.
  • The venue address, your ticket QR code and your voting link are sent by email after your purchase. The address is not published.

Your ticket

  • Your ticket is the QR code in your confirmation email. Show it on your phone at the door. Nothing is printed and nothing is mailed. It scans once. A screenshot of someone else's ticket does not get you in.
  • Lost the email? Reply to your confirmation or write blindtastetest@reservethereserve.com from the email you bought with.
  • Every testing pass includes hardware at the station, a merch item at check in, and your votes on your phone. The judge code under the QR on your ticket unlocks the voting app. One code, one day. Submit and your result is on your screen and in your inbox that second.

Changes

  • No refunds. All sales are final at purchase.
  • Transfers: you can change the name on a ticket until Oct 7 by emailing blindtastetest@reservethereserve.com from the purchasing email with the new name. No name changes after Oct 7. No transfers at the door.
  • If the event is cancelled by the organizers, tickets are refunded in full. If a date moves, your ticket moves with it, and you can request a refund within 7 days of the announcement.

House rules you agree to

  • What the pass buys: your place at the testing stations, hardware at the station, your ticket QR on your phone, your scorecard on your phone, a merch item, the programming and building access for the day. Nothing else is sold on site.
  • Consumption indoors only. Never on a balcony or at a window.
  • Dry event. The bar is NA all weekend.
  • Follow staff and security direction. Anyone removed for behavior forfeits their ticket with no refund.
  • Passing the test: every resin or rosin vote you cast correct. If more than one judge passes, the prize vault is valued at MSRP and split equally between the passers.
  • The organizers may photograph and film the event. Buying a ticket is consent to appear in recap content. Tell the shooter if you want to be skipped.
  • No one under 21 in the building, including infants.

Parking and getting there

  • Parking all around the building: the Parkwell lot behind the building, parking garages everywhere, street parking. All paid, all first come. Your parking is your responsibility, not ours. Rideshare is the move. Three blocks from Union Station rail.
  • Flying in: RTD A Line train from DEN to Union Station, 37 minutes, $10.50, every 15 minutes. Walk to your hotel or the venue.
  • Seven hotels within a short walk of the venue, which sits half a block from Coors Field and three blocks from Union Station. Book direct: The Rally at McGregor Square, The Maven at Dairy Block, The Oxford, The Crawford (inside Union Station), Thompson Denver, Kimpton Hotel Born, Hotel Indigo Union Station, Hilton Garden Inn Union Station, Kimpton Hotel Monaco, The Brown Palace. 2. Internal rules (door, inbox, refunds desk)

  • Name matching. Ticket name must match ID. If it does not, the buyer emails a name change before Oct 7 or the ticket is refused. Door lead can override for obvious typos only, logged.
  • Reused QR. Scanner says Already used. Do not admit. Offer to look up the order. If the original buyer is inside, the second person is not getting in on that ticket.
  • No QR, lost email. Look up by name in the Commerce order list, check ID, check the ticket in manually by ID. Every manual check-in gets logged with the staffer's name.
  • Wrong day. A Sunday pass at the Saturday door is valid but wrong. Scanner flags amber. Send them back Sunday. Do not swap days at the door, Saturday is capped.
  • Refund requests. Standard answer is no. Exceptions only for organizer cancellation or date move. Duplicate purchase by the same buyer within 24 hours: refund the duplicate, that is a mistake not a change of mind.
  • Chargebacks. Reply to PayPal with the order, the no-refund line at checkout, the ticket email and the scan record if they attended.
  • Transfers. Name changes are done in the Commerce order (or Verbb purchased ticket) and a new ticket email with a fresh QR goes to the buyer. The old QR is dead after the change.
  • Preparty. Separate list, separate scan, separate door at Explore Glass Gallery. GA cap is whatever the gallery gives us.
  • Capacity. 150 in the building. Live count on the scanner phones, with staff, sponsor and vendor wristbands counted off the named list at the door.
  • Judge code. Under the QR in the ticket email and in the voting link. Lost it: look up the order, read the code off the Tickets tab. A code that has already submitted its votes cannot score again.
  • Sponsor and vendor credentials. Wristband, not a ticket. Named list at the door, ID required, no scan.
  • Competitor credentials. Two per entry, four for a brand entering two or more strains. Competitor wristband, named list, ID required, no scan. Not a judge pass, they do not take the test on it.
