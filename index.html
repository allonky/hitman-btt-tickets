The test

You are the judge.

Your ticket makes you an official judge.

Start by visiting the display case, the only place you’ll see the numbered entries. Then head to any of ten testing stations throughout the venue. Choose a number, put on the blindfold and let a house dabtender serve your sample from a sealed jar.

No labels. No brands. No visual clues. Just your palate.

After each dab, remove the blindfold and vote on your phone using the judge code printed on your ticket. Leave your tasting notes, score the flavor from 1 to 10 and make the call: live resin or live rosin.

Voting closes at 6 PM. Then the master key is revealed onstage, the entries are unmasked and the winners are announced.

Ten stations. Every entry. No endless line. Test at your own pace throughout the day.

You are in

Your tickets

They are also in your inbox. Your phone is the ticket: show the QR and an ID with the same name at the door.

Hardware at every station

All ten stations are equipped with rigs, e-rigs and glass. Everything you need is provided. Just show up.

Vote from your phone

Your ticket is a QR code included in your confirmation email. Nothing is printed or mailed. Scan it at the door, collect your wristband and use the same phone to cast your vote for every entry.

Voting is a web app

The code on your ticket unlocks our voting app. Each entry has its own row, where you can score flavor from 1 to 10, select resin or rosin and add notes. Your choices save automatically with every tap. Prefer a larger screen? An iPad voting station will be available on the floor.

Instant results

As soon as you submit your ballot, your score appears on your phone, showing how many of your votes matched the results. We’ll also email you a copy to screenshot and share. Passers and winners are determined directly from the scores and announced that night. Nothing is counted by hand.
