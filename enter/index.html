The ask

Twenty four grams. One strain.

  • 24 grams per entry. Live resin or live rosin, one strain, from your own line. That covers 200 judges at ten stations across two days.
  • Enter more than once if you want. Every strain is its own entry with its own 24 grams. Submit the form once per entry.
  • Licensed Colorado brands only. We need your MED license number on the form, the one issued by the Colorado Marijuana Enforcement Division. Every entry is pennied out for the contest at KrystaLeaves dispensary, 5301 Vasquez Blvd, Unit 101, Commerce City, CO 80022.
  • You stay anonymous on the floor. Jars are numbered. Judges never see a label. Your name comes out when the key drops and the winners are announced.
  • We confirm every entry by email. Drop off details, the deadline and the paperwork come in that email. Questions before you enter: blindtastetest@reservethereserve.com.
  • Twenty to thirty entries total. When the list is full it is full.

How the test works

Judges buy a $200 pass for Saturday or Sunday. At every station they pick a number, put on the blindfold, and a house dabtender serves your sample from a sealed jar. They score flavor 1 to 10, vote live resin or live rosin, and move on.

Best Taste

Highest flavor score across both days. Consumer choice, 200 judges, no panel. Trophy to the company, announced at the Sunday results ceremony.
